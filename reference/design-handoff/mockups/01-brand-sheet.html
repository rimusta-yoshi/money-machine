05 · Voice

We say "Pick your trade. We'll do the rest." "Nice, that's your services sorted." "£99 once. That's it." "This layout needs a photo. Add one, or we'll use this instead."
We don't say "Leverage our AI-powered platform." "Error: validation failed." "Starting from £99*" "Unlock premium features."

Upbeat, short and on your side. Talk like a mate who's good with computers, not like a tech company. Celebrate small wins, never blame, and always say the price plainly.
