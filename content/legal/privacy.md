## Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted services to run {{brand}}. They only use your data to provide their service to us:

- **Cloudflare:** hosting, storage and security for the builder and all websites.
- **Stripe:** card payments, refunds and disputes. Stripe also uses payment data under its own privacy policy, for example to prevent fraud.
- **Resend:** sending our emails (receipts, edit links, refund confirmations).
- **Google (Gmail):** our support inbox.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to deal with fraud, abuse or illegal content.

Some of these companies store data outside the UK, including in the USA. When they do, the transfer is protected by safeguards approved under UK law, such as the UK–US data bridge or UK-approved contract clauses.

## Cookies and browser storage

We don't use tracking or advertising cookies. The builder saves your progress in your own browser so you don't lose your work; this is needed for the builder to work. Our homepage uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without identifying you. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may set a short-lived security cookie to tell real visitors from bots; it isn't used for tracking or advertising.

Websites made with {{brand}} don't use tracking or advertising cookies either.

## Visitors to websites made with {{brand}}

Each website belongs to the business that made it, and that business is responsible for what it says. When someone visits one, Cloudflare processes their IP address to deliver the page securely. We don't track visitors or build profiles of them.
