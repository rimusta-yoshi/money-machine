# Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about paying, what you get, and what happens later. Can't find yours? Email [{{email}}](mailto:{{email}}).

## Paying once

### Is it really {{price}} once, with no monthly fee?

Yes. You pay {{price}} when you're happy with your site, and that's it. No subscription, no renewal, no add-ons. Hosting is included for as long as {{brand}} runs.

### Why so cheap, when other builders charge every month?

A simple website for a local business costs very little to keep online. We'd rather charge a fair price once than a small amount every month, forever. You can read the full reasoning on [our homepage](/).

### When do I pay?

Only at the end. Building your site is free, and you can try every look first. You pay on the last step, Go live, and your site goes online straight away. Card payments are handled by Stripe; your card details never reach us.

## What's included

### What do I get for {{price}}?

- A website made with our builder, with your services, photos, areas and contact details.
- Your own web address: your-name.{{domain}}.
- Hosting, for as long as {{brand}} runs.
- Free changes whenever you like, with your edit link.
- A download of your whole site whenever you want it.

Not included: a domain name of your own, email accounts, or custom design work. The [terms](/terms/) list everything.

### Can I use my own domain name?

Yes, by hosting your site yourself: download it, put it on a free host, and point your domain at it. Our guide, [Host your own site](/guide/host-your-own-site/), walks through it step by step. We don't connect your own domain to our hosting yet; your site with us lives at your-name.{{domain}}.

### Is there a "Made with {{brand}}" link on my site?

Yes, a small one at the very bottom. It helps other local businesses find us.

## Changing your site

### Can I change my site later?

Yes, as often as you like, free. We email you an edit link when you pay. Open it on any device, change your text, photos or layout, and press Publish changes.

### What if I lose my edit link?

Ask for a new one from the builder ("Already paid? Get your edit link"). It's sent to the email address you paid with, and opening it stops the older links working.

### Can I download my site?

Yes. Press **Download your site** on the Go live step (opened with your edit link), or use the link in your welcome email. You get a .zip with your whole site: pages, photos and fonts. It opens straight from your computer, and you can put it on any host.

## Refunds

### What if I'm not happy?

Use the "Request a refund" link in your welcome email within {{guaranteeDays}} days of paying. You get the full {{price}} back, no questions asked, and your site is taken down. It's self-serve, so you don't need to wait for us.

## The future

### What happens if {{brand}} closes?

We'd email you at least 90 days before, so you have plenty of time to download your site and move it. Your site is plain files, so it works on any host, and [our guide](/guide/host-your-own-site/) shows how to move it for free.

### Will you add my type of business?

We add new types of business every month. If yours isn't listed, start with the closest one and change the wording to fit. On the builder's first step, press "My business isn't listed" and tell us what you do: it helps us choose what to add next.
