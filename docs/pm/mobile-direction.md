# Mobile builder: agreed direction (13–14 Oct 2026)

Clickable prototype (rob's account): https://claude.ai/artifact/Y1RxThgephzTk114jTce3y

Why: most ad traffic will be phones. Problems today: section pills cover site content (worst on thin strips); the bottom sheet takes a quarter to half the screen and, with the keyboard open, hides the site; editing and choosing on one screen is too much.

## Flow (per section)
1. Swipe layouts. Bar: ‹  Edit <section>  ›, a line "Hero · Photo on top · 1 of 6 · swipe to change", and a brick "Next: <section>" button. Whatever layout is showing is the choice: no "use this layout" confirmation.
2. "Edit <section>" opens just that section's fields; only that section stays visible above them, updating live as you type. Sheet never hides the section; with the keyboard open the preview scrolls so the edited bit stays visible.
3. Done moves straight on to the next section. "Next" skips editing when the pre-filled content is fine.
4. Tapping an earlier section returns to it with the slim bar (swipe + Edit), not the form.
5. No pills on the site: the section name lives in the bar; the selected section only gets an outline.

## Content details
- Hero headline: 3 suggested headlines (varied per site, include trade/town) + "write your own" (max ~60 chars). Page title/meta/LocalBusiness data stay generated from Basics, so edits can't hurt SEO; varied headlines reduce duplicate copy across customer sites.
- Photos: "Add your photo" is prominent; no "keep sample" button; "No photo to hand? Carry on. We'll remind you before you go live." Go live nudge + no-photo layout fallback stay.
- After upload: tap the important part (focal point) so crops keep it; a Phone / Computer preview toggle on the edit screen; generator prefers portrait-friendly hero layouts for tall photos.
- Banned-word check (e.g. bank, login, verify your account, password, crypto) flags a site for rob's review; sites have no forms that collect credentials or payments.
- "Email me a link to finish on my computer": saves the build (seed, picks, content, photo refs) server-side and emails a resume link; covered by the 30-day unpaid-copy cleanup and privacy policy. Optional single reminder only if they tick "remind me".
