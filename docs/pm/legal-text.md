## PRIVACY POLICY (page: /privacy)

*Last updated: [DATE]*

We collect only what we need to build, host and support your website. We don't use tracking cookies, we don't sell your data, and we don't share it for advertising.

### Who we are

Local Blocks is run by [FULL NAME], a sole trader, at [BUSINESS ADDRESS]. For anything about your data, email hello@localblocks.co.uk. We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), registration number [ICO REGISTRATION NUMBER].

### What we collect and why

| What | Examples | Why | Legal basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Your site content | Business name, trade, town, phone number, services, about text, photos | To build, show and publish your website | Contract (providing the service) |
| Your email address | The email you give at Go live | To send your receipt, edit link and refund link, and to answer you | Contract |
| Payment details | Card payments, handled by Stripe. We see the amount, date, email and card country, never your full card number | To take payment, give refunds and handle disputes | Contract; legal obligation (tax records) |
| Support messages | Emails you send to hello@ or abuse@ | To help you and deal with reports | Legitimate interests (running a support service) |
| Anonymous usage counts | Which builder steps are reached, which layouts are picked, which ad a visit came from. No names, emails or IP addresses | To understand where people get stuck and improve the builder | Legitimate interests (improving the service) |
| Technical data | IP address and browser details, seen by our hosting provider when pages load | To deliver pages and protect against attacks and abuse | Legitimate interests (security) |

Your phone number, business name and other site content are meant to be public once your site is live. Only add what you are happy for anyone to see.

### Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted services to run Local Blocks. They only use your data to provide their service to us:

- **Cloudflare:** hosting, storage and security for the builder and all websites.
- **Stripe:** card payments, refunds and disputes. Stripe also uses payment data under its own privacy policy, for example to prevent fraud.
- **Resend:** sending our emails (receipts, edit links, refund confirmations).
- **Google (Gmail):** our support inbox.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to deal with fraud, abuse or illegal content.

Some of these companies store data outside the UK, including in the USA. When they do, the transfer is protected by safeguards approved under UK law, such as the UK–US data bridge or UK-approved contract clauses.

### How long we keep it

| Data | Kept for |
| --- | --- |
| Builder progress saved in your browser | On your device until you clear it. We can't see it |
| Unpaid copies (previews, abandoned checkouts) and their photos | 30 days, then deleted |
| A live website and its content | While your site is live |
| A refunded or removed website | Taken down straight away, deleted after 90 days |
| Payment and tax records | 6 years, as UK tax law requires |
| Support emails | 2 years after the conversation ends |
| Anonymous usage counts | 13 months |

### Cookies and browser storage

We don't use tracking or advertising cookies. The builder saves your progress in your own browser so you don't lose your work; this is needed for the builder to work. Our homepage uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without identifying you. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may set a short-lived security cookie to tell real visitors from bots; it isn't used for tracking or advertising.

Websites made with Local Blocks don't use tracking or advertising cookies either.

### Visitors to websites made with Local Blocks

Each website belongs to the business that made it, and that business is responsible for what it says. When someone visits one, Cloudflare processes their IP address to deliver the page securely. We don't track visitors or build profiles of them.

### Your rights

You can ask us to:

- give you a copy of your data
- correct anything wrong
- delete your data (we'll keep payment records for as long as tax law requires)
- stop or limit how we use it, or object to it
- give you your data in a format you can take elsewhere

Email hello@localblocks.co.uk. We'll reply within one month. It's free.

### Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

### Age

Local Blocks is for businesses and adults. It isn't meant for anyone under 18.

### Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your data, we'll email you first.
